About Me

Educator &
Researcher

I am Dr. Abhijeet Dhore, a dedicated educator and researcher with a passion for advancing knowledge and inspiring students. My academic journey has been driven by a commitment to excellence in both teaching and scholarly inquiry.

My research interests span Cosmology, Dark Energy models, and Modified Theories of Gravity, and I strive to bridge the gap between theoretical foundations and real-world applications. I believe in fostering a learning environment that encourages curiosity, critical thinking, and intellectual growth.

With years of teaching experience, I have guided hundreds of students through complex concepts, helping them develop both technical skills and analytical mindsets that serve them throughout their careers.
